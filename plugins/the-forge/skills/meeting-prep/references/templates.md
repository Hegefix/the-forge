# Meeting prep templates

## The page

```markdown
# <Meeting>, <date> <time>, with <team or people>

Context: <two lines: why the meeting, what we want out of it>.

## Facts

- <fact> (source: <file:line | ticket key, status | version, date | decision-log line>)
- <fact> (not confirmed: <what would confirm it>)

## Options

1. <option>: <what it costs, what it gives>. **Recommended**, because <reason>.
2. <option>: <trade-off>.

## Questions for them

- <question>

## Five lines to say

1. <one sentence>
2. …
```

## A ticket draft for another team's board

Copy the field names and body sections from their own recent tickets; these are placeholders.

```markdown
### <Summary, in their naming style>

Fields: type <…>, parent <epic key>, labels <…>, component <…>, team <…>, sprint <proposed>, assignee <proposed | unassigned>.

<Body in their sections, e.g.:>
**Goal:** <one line>.
**Do:**
1. <step>
**Done when:**
- <check>
**Links:** <designs, related tickets>.
```
